Extension: Caloric Restriction

Official URL: https://openanimalnutrition.org/fhir/animal-nutrition/StructureDefinition/caloric-restriction Version: 0.1.0
Draft as of 2026-10-26 Computable Name: CaloricRestriction
Other Identifiers: OID:2.16.840.1.113883.4.642.40.1004.42.6

The intentional caloric restriction that the check was run under, as a percentage of the animal's energy need, in UCUM %: 25 means that the animal is fed 25% less energy than it needs. Most of the applied standard's per-Mcal minimums are then multiplied by 1 ÷ (1 − restriction ÷ 100) (under an AAFCO selection, together with any AAFCO dry matter factor), so a nutrient can be deficient although it meets the minimum that applies with no restriction. The source template accepts 0 (none) or a number from 1 to 55. Present only for a restriction above 0, and absent when none was entered (blank or 0 in the template). An intake entered in place of the calculated energy requirement is not a restriction. Neither it nor the template's figure for how far it falls below that requirement (cell N4 on the template's Standards tab) is recorded here. Which minimums are raised, and the rule that makes a value below them deficient, are stated, proposed and pending ratification, on the Decile Basis and Band Computation page.
