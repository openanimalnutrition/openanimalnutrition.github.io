This page is for readers who use the source template's data without its cell notes: exports, programs and AI checkers. It gives the template's status and its known limitations, as the template's own status block states them (Standards tab, cell A2 and rows 132 to 149, Version 21OCT26c), and the errata for copies of earlier versions. The template's cell notes named below give the details. How the template computes the bands, and the proposed rules for a verdict, are on the Decile Basis and Band Computation page.

Status

The source template is the Standardized Nutrient Ranges | Dynamic Decile System Table. Its last version, 21OCT26c, has been live since 25 October 2026: the final feature version, 21OCT26, with the wording of four texts corrected (21OCT26b) and its status text updated (21OCT26c). In the template's words (cells A133 to A135):

Status: this is the final feature version of this sheet. It is frozen: only errors are corrected, each with a new version label and a row on the History of Change(s) sheet; no new inputs, requirements, rules or standards editions are added. Requests for new features are taken for the code version, not for this sheet.

The code version, the Open Animal Nutrition checker, is live at https://openanimalnutrition.org/ (for people) and https://checker.openanimalnutrition.org/mcp (for AI systems, no sign-in). For each nutrient it names the facts a result depends on that it does not take as inputs (e.g., age, breed, whether a nutrient is added as an additive).

Since 24 Oct 2026 (checker release 0.5.0), the checker, not this sheet, is the reference implementation of the computation proposed by Open Animal Nutrition; its verdicts deficient and excess remain the provisional steward's proposal, pending ratification. Run with this sheet's rules, it reproduces this sheet. Its own rules, where they differ from this sheet's and why, and the Open Animal Nutrition FHIR Implementation Guide are at https://github.com/openanimalnutrition.

  • The template is retired. It is no longer changed, although cell A133 still describes error corrections: corrections are made in the checker and recorded in its changes document.
  • The code version is the reference. The code version (Python), the checker, run with the template's rules, which it keeps for this check, gives the template's results on its released test cases, and the provisional steward approved, on 22 October 2026, each case where it gives a different result, with its reason. It has been the reference since 24 October 2026, from its release 0.5.0. Its own rules that differ from the template's, set on 23 and 24 October 2026, are recorded with their reasons in its changes document. It reads its requirement values, each with its source, from its data folder.
  • The adequacy interpretation stays proposed. Which values are deficient or excess is the provisional steward's proposal, pending ratification by the governance body, in the code version as on the Decile Basis and Band Computation page.
  • Errors in a value or a rule are corrected in the checker: report them with the issue forms in the checker's repository. Differences between the checker and this guide can be reported in the guide's source repository.

Standards

In the template's words (cell A136):

Standards: 2006 NRC Nutrient Requirements of Dogs and Cats; 2026 AAFCO Official Publication (Dog and Cat Food Nutrient Profiles); FEDIAF Nutritional Guidelines, September 2025.

This guide's Requirement Standards code system has a code for each edition: NRC-2006, AAFCO-2026 and FEDIAF-2025.

What the descriptions show

In the template's words (cells A137 and A138):

The descriptors compare the entered values with the selected requirement's ranges only; they do not show that a diet is adequate, safe or compliant (see the L1 and G1 notes).

A value above a range high reads Highest. The Open Animal Nutrition FHIR IG calls a value excess only above an authoritative max (max types in columns AU to BO of the Healthy Dog Cat Reqt Data sheet; see the E5 note); this grouping is proposed, pending ratification.

The grouping is under Maximum types.

Known limitations

The template has no input for the conditions below. In the template's words (cells A139 to A149; the cell notes named are on the Standards tab unless stated):

Known limitations (this sheet has no input for them; details in the notes named):

  1. Age: FEDIAF Dog Late Growth puppies with an expected adult weight over 15 kg keep the calcium min of 2.5 g/Mcal and the Ca:P max of 1.6 after about 6 months of age, when FEDIAF allows 2.0 and 1.8 (AE52 and AF56 notes on the Healthy Dog Cat Reqt Data sheet).
  2. Breed: FEDIAF's preferred calcium max for Great Dane puppies (1.6% DM) is not applied (B63 note).
  3. Added nutrients: FEDIAF's EU legal maxs for copper, iodine, iron, manganese, selenium and zinc apply only to amounts added as additives, but are applied here to every diet (J3 note on the Healthy Dog Cat Reqt Data sheet).
  4. Sources: iron added as oxide or carbonate and copper added as oxide should not be counted toward the mins (B78 and B80 notes); limits on added selenium and on phosphorus from soluble sources (cats) are not checked (B89 and B67 notes).
  5. Conditional mins: the cat vitamin K and biotin mins apply to every diet, although the standards make them conditional (B102 and B116 notes).
  6. Reference animals: NRC's per kg body weight^0.67 values for kittens and lactating queens apply to one reference animal; this sheet uses them for every kitten or queen (AG3 and AQ3 notes on the Healthy Dog Cat Reqt Data sheet).
  7. Moisture: foods with more than 12% moisture count as wet, a lower cut than FEDIAF's 14% (B40 note); FEDIAF's wet-diet selenium values per kg BW^0.75 (dogs) or BW^0.67 (cats) are not used (B91 note).
  8. Energy density: under an AAFCO requirement, the mins of a food below 4 kcal/g DM are raised to AAFCO's dry matter basis but its maxs stay per Mcal, so it can read above a max it meets on a dry matter basis (C130 note).
  9. Not calculated, only stated in notes: FEDIAF's increase of essential amino acids below 80% protein digestibility and the protein needs of bitches fed little carbohydrate (B12), vitamin E for high PUFA or fish oil diets (B100), vitamin B6 with protein in cats (B112), trace minerals at high calcium (B85), magnesium and urine pH in cats (B76), and processing and storage losses (G1 and B104).
  10. This sheet's own choices where the standards give no value: range highs derived from the min (K1 and K72 notes; Exceptions to Range High sheet), the carbohydrate default min (C124), the energy density range (C130), the energy estimates for the AAFCO requirements (G4 note on the Healthy Dog Cat Reqt Data sheet) and the folate DFE conversion (B114).

For the conflicts found in applying the standards' figures and the stated rules, see Conflicts for the governance body.

Errata for copies of earlier versions

Copies of the template stay in use after a new version is released. A copy's version label is in its cell A1. Known errors in earlier versions:

  • NRC puppy and bitch values per kg BW^0.75 (Version 18OCT26 and earlier). Under NRC RA Puppy 4-14 Wk Growth, NRC RA Puppy 14 Wk+ Growth and NRC RA Dog Late Gestation & Peak Lactation, the MBW rows hold NRC's values per kg BW^0.75. NRC states these for one reference animal only (a 5.5 kg puppy of 35 kg expected mature body weight; a 22 kg bitch in peak lactation with 8 puppies, eating 5,000 kcal ME a day), and calculates other animals' needs from the per-1,000 kcal values. The template converts them with each animal's own energy, so they misread. For example, a 15 kg bitch at the template's default inputs (late gestation energy, 1,380.9 kcal a day) fed exactly her per-Mcal minimums reads Severely Restricted on all 42 of her MBW rows (protein MBW minimum 135.8 against 50 g/Mcal), and her vitamin D MBW maximum reads 2,163.7 against NRC's 800 IU/Mcal safe upper limit. For puppies the MBW minimums read about 0.5 to 2.2 times the per-Mcal minimums (from 10% to 100% of the expected mature body weight), and above about 88% of the expected mature body weight the calcium MBW minimum passes NRC's 4.5 g/Mcal safe upper limit, so no diet meets both rows. In such a copy, use the per-Mcal rows under these three selections and disregard their MBW rows, or use Version 21OCT26c, which has no MBW values for them.
  • Wording of four texts (Version 21OCT26). Status items 6 and 7 (cells A145 and A146) and the notes on cells AG3 and AQ3 of the Healthy Dog Cat Reqt Data tab say per kg body weight for values per kg BW^0.75 or BW^0.67. No value or formula is affected. Version 21OCT26b corrects the wording.

The template's History of Change(s) tab lists every change in each version, and the Decile Basis and Band Computation page summarizes the changes in Versions 21OCT26, 21OCT26b and 21OCT26c.
