Extension: Nutrient Decile (Common-Basis Expression)

Official URL: https://openanimalnutrition.org/fhir/animal-nutrition/StructureDefinition/nutrient-decile Version: 0.1.0
Draft as of 2026-10-26 Computable Name: NutrientDecile
Other Identifiers: OID:2.16.840.1.113883.4.642.40.1004.42.4

The diet's level for this nutrient on the common mathematical basis used to compare across standards, expressed as a band of the Dynamic Decile System: four bands below the minimum and ten deciles from the minimum to the range high. Where an entry gets a band, a value above the range high is in range-d10 (Highest) unless it is below the minimum. The decile basis and band computation are stated on the Decile Basis and Band Computation page, proposed and pending ratification by the governance body.

Context of Use

    This extension may be used on the following element(s)
